The pattern is consistent across the seven definitions: the /resources/definitions/ copy is roughly twice as long and carries an April publish date, while the shorter root copy was published in June. The pages were rebuilt at the root without retiring the originals. The four guides split three-to-one the other way, so those need deciding individually.

Recommended

301 the losing copy to the winner in each pair through the Redirection plugin — it takes effect immediately and needs no snippet, which matters given the decision to stop adding WPCode. Then drop the retired URLs from the sitemap. Do the merchandising pair first; it is the only one touching a term the account is actively trying to win.

Two known issues, with corrected numbers

Both were logged on August 27 as "known issues, no action yet". Measuring them properly changed what each one actually is.

ItemWas logged asMeasured today
Post types with no Yoast head output "62 case study pages are silent to search" 62 URLs is right, but only 34 of them are case studies — that is the entire post type. The rest are 23 /troc-videos/, 4 /ebook-papers/ and /lifeattroc/. All 62 carry no canonical and no JSON-LD; 36 also have no meta description. Confirmed by re-fetching all 62 individually. So it is not one post type misconfigured but three, which points at a Yoast content-type setting rather than at the Webflow plugin.
Pages holding a second HTML document "11 pages hold an entire second HTML document … producing the 9 duplicate canonicals under /resources/" The count of 11 is exact and confirmed: two <title> and two <!doctype> on each. The part worth acting on: the August cleanup produced CLEAN-*.html files and applied them to the root copies, but the nine /resources/ copies are the ones actually in the sitemap and were never cleaned. Three other /resources/ guides — automated retail, clienteling analytics, assembly services — are clean, so this is a bounded set.

Sequencing note

These two overlap with the duplicate-pairs finding above. Settle which copy of each pair survives before stripping nested HTML, or you will clean pages you are about to redirect away.
