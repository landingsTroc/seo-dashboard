Of the 146 URLs in the Ahrefs "title too long" export, 120 are fixed and live.
The 26 below could NOT be fixed through the API. All of them share one cause:
their <title> is not produced by Yoast, so writing _yoast_wpseo_title has no
effect on the rendered page.

  19 case studies — Yoast is switched OFF for the case-studies post type.
     These pages emit no canonical, no schema and no og:title either, and the
     WordPress post title is only the brand name ("Apple"). The long title
     comes from the theme/ACF layer, which the REST API does not expose.
     The right fix is to enable Yoast for this post type: it closes the title
     problem AND the missing canonicals and schema in one change. Needs admin.

   6 video pages — Yoast emits nothing on these six specific /troc-videos/
     URLs while it works normally on the other 13. Same symptom, cause not yet
     identified.

     new title, but a filter downstream replaces it before output. Not in the
     WPCode snippets backed up on 2026-08-27, so it needs a live look at the
     snippet list. Needs admin (the marketing_writer account cannot read the
     wpcode post type).
