Technical cleanup

1,029 redirect links removed from page content. The remaining 5,100 sit in four places the API cannot reach.

Ahrefs flagged 6,146 internal links pointing at URLs that redirect across 800 URLs. Every one inside post and page content is now fixed and verified. The rest is not a content problem: it is seven links in the site chrome, and each of those is counted once per page it appears on, which is why they dominate the total. Three of my own earlier conclusions were wrong and are corrected at the bottom.

Redirecting links inside content went from 1,032 to 3. The three that remain are deliberate: they need a content decision, not a rewrite. Every link now points at a destination verified to return 200 in a single hop — all 22 replacement URLs were checked before any edit was made.

How each write was verified

Each record was fetched, transformed in memory, checked against an invariant — no link may disappear except a known redirect or a dead one — then written and read back and compared byte for byte. Two sets of backups sit on disk: the original content and the pre-write live state. The invariant caught a genuine bug before it shipped: an early version of the list-item removal would have deleted 41 links to live pages.
